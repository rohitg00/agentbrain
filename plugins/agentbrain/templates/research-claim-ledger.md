# Research Claim Ledger

Schema fields: `question`, `claims`, `sources`, `freshness`, `contradictions`, `confidence`, `recheck_trigger`, `next_action`.

## Question

State the claim, API, market signal, or current fact being checked.

## Claims

| Claim | Source provenance | Evidence checked | Confidence | Recheck trigger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  |  |  |  |  |

## Sources

List inspected source URLs, files, docs, or logs with dates or commits.

## Contradictions

Name conflicts, stale evidence, or unsupported claims.

## Freshness

Record date, commit, expiry, and what would make the evidence stale.

## Decision

State whether the evidence supports, weakens, blocks, or redirects the work.

## Next Action

Route to the next command, skill, or stop condition.
