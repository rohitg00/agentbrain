# Launch Checklist

Schema fields: `change`, `go_no_go`, `validation`, `ci_status`, `rollback`, `monitoring`, `support`, `approvals`, `risks`, `next_action`.

## Change

Describe what is being merged, released, deployed, published, or otherwise exposed.

## Go/No-Go Decision

State `go`, `no-go`, or `blocked` with evidence.

## Validation

List local tests, validators, scrub, review, and CI proof.

## Rollback

State the exact rollback path and owner.

## Monitoring

Name signals, logs, checks, or alerts to watch after launch.

## Support Path

Document who or what handles user issues, incidents, and follow-up.

## Approvals

Record explicit approval evidence for side effects or public changes.

## Risks

Name remaining product, security, reliability, or maintenance risks.

## Next Action

Ship, block, rollback, monitor, or collect missing evidence.
