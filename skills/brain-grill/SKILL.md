# Brain Grill

## Trigger

Use before committing to a product direction, architecture, UX, launch, or large implementation.

## Procedure

1. Classify what is being grilled: product, design, engineering, launch, or strategy.
2. Run the seven grills from `docs/grilling-protocol.md`.
3. Separate must-fix from should-fix.
4. Propose a smaller sharper v0.
5. State what evidence would change the recommendation.

## Pitfalls

- Do not confuse criticism with cynicism.
- Do not reject an idea without a better smaller option.
- Do not accept “everyone” as a target user.

## Verification

The user should know exactly what is weak, what to answer, and what v0 to build.
