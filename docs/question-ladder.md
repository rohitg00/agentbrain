# Question Ladder

Use this before planning or implementation. The agent should ask only the questions that materially change the next action.

## Level 1 — Intent

- What are we trying to make possible for the user?
- Who is the primary user?
- What pain, job, or desire triggers this?
- What should be easier, faster, safer, cheaper, or more delightful?

## Level 2 — Context

- Where will this live?
- What already exists?
- What constraints are real?
- What constraints are preferences?
- What must not change?

## Level 3 — Outcome

- What does success look like in one sentence?
- What measurable signal proves it worked?
- What is the smallest demo that would convince us?
- What would make us kill or pause the idea?

## Level 4 — User Journey

- What happens before the user arrives?
- What is the first action?
- Where can they get stuck?
- What does a magical moment look like?
- What happens after success?

## Level 5 — Risk

- What can break technically?
- What can confuse users?
- What can create security, privacy, compliance, or trust issues?
- What can become expensive or slow?
- What assumption is most likely false?

## Level 6 — Scope

- What is in v0?
- What is explicitly out of v0?
- What can be mocked?
- What must be real?
- What should be reversible?

## Question budget rule

If the user is blocked by too many questions, ask the top 3 only and proceed with labeled assumptions.
