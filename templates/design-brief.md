# Design Brief

Schema fields: `user`, `workflow`, `states`, `interfaces`, `edge_cases`, `accessibility`, `failure_paths`, `evidence_checked`, `risks`, `next_action`.

## User and Job

Define the user, job-to-be-done, and non-goals.

## Workflow

Describe the happy path and key decision points.

## States

List states, transitions, empty/error/loading conditions, and stop conditions.

## Interfaces

Name screens, commands, APIs, artifacts, or handoff surfaces.

## Edge Cases

Document boundary cases, retries, permissions, and fallback behavior.

## Evidence Checked

List source files, user evidence, examples, docs, or sketches inspected.

## Risks

Call out unclear requirements, safety, privacy, accessibility, or maintainability risks.

## Next Action

Route to planning, review, or another design slice.
